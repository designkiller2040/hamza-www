Opening performance for Cold Rush exhibition - Berlin 2025

Сold rush traces the silent intensity of Berlin’s winter, season of solitude, trance, and creation. Emerging from long nights of dances, the works reflect a state where perception blurs and time stretches into trance.

In this state, the artist confronts the cold not as absence, but as a generative force: the cold touch of metal against skin, the pulse of energy that moves through the body and into form.

The paintings carry traces of this season its silence, its density, and its strange luminosity. They oscillate between abstraction and sensation, between the blur of nightlife and the clarity that follows it. Cold Rush speaks to that suspended moment when exhaustion gives way to creation, when the boundaries of self and environment dissolve in rhythm and repetition Presented in spring, cold rush stands as a release from that compressed winter energy. Between abstraction and atmosphere, the works mirror the experience of transition: from night to dawn, from isolation to renewal, from the metallic cold of Berlin to the first warmth of light.
