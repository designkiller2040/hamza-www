"Abimal is this subjective, plural visual truth that invites visitors to trust their own eyes: what you see is your truth."

Hamza Shah (b. 1999) is a Swiss-Afghan multidisciplinary artist currently based in Berlin, Germany, whose practice spans painting, installation, and sound.

Hamza reflects on alignement, motherhood and belonging, themes rooted in both his personal and cultural background. His works evoke calm, stillness, and presence, embodying a meditative rhythm that mirrors his process of slowing down and listening inward.

For him, creating is a physical act a dialogue between hand, matter, and emotion. Each piece becomes a way to return to what is real, to something primitive, as he describes it, an antidote to the noise of the modern world.
